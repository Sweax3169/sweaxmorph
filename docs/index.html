Questions

Does it upload my files?

No. Everything happens on your PC. The only download is FFmpeg, Pandoc or 7-Zip, once, from their official releases, the first time a conversion needs them.

Why isn't it in the new Windows 11 menu?

That menu only accepts packaged, signed apps. SweaxMorph adds a classic entry, which Windows 11 shows under "Show more options" (Shift + F10). The shortcut and Space-while-dragging are usually faster anyway.

Do I need Office?

No. Office is used when it is there because it is the most faithful. Without it, LibreOffice or Pandoc do the job, and most kinds of files never need either.

Will it overwrite my files?

Never. Results go next to the original as "name (2).ext" when the name is taken, or to the Desktop or a folder you choose.

Where does Morph open?

On the monitor your pointer is on, at that monitor's own scale: next to the pointer, in the middle of the screen, or where you last left it (drag the cloud around Morph to move it). If Morph is already waiting on another monitor, the shortcut brings it over instead of hiding it.

Does it work over games and administrator windows?

Over full-screen windows, yes: Morph stays on top. Administrator windows (Task Manager, installers, many games) keep their keys from ordinary apps, so turn on "Also over administrator windows" in Settings: a tiny helper with administrator rights then hears the shortcut there too. Windows asks once.

Is it free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT license.
