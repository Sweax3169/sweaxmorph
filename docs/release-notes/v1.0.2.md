**SweaxMorph 1.0.2**

- **Morph waits for you.** Opening Morph and then clicking a file in Explorer to drag it in made Morph leave, because it closed whenever it lost focus. Now it stays on top while you go and pick the file: its haze fades so the windows under it can be clicked, and comes back as soon as a drag heads its way. It closes with the new close button, <kbd>Esc</kbd>, the shortcut again, or a few seconds after a finished conversion.
- **Settings and close buttons** on Morph's top-right. Settings holds the shortcut (click *Change* and press new keys), the Explorer options, where results go, quality and the engines; until now it was only in the tray menu.
- **Dozes when idle.** After a few quiet seconds Morph closes its eyes and stops drawing frames, so leaving it open costs no CPU. It wakes when the pointer comes near.

**Türkçe:** Morph artık sen Gezgin'de dosya seçerken kapanmıyor: üstte bekliyor, etrafındaki sis çekiliyor ki altındaki pencerelere tıklayabilesin, dosyayı sürükleyip yaklaştırınca sis geri geliyor. Sağ üstünde Ayarlar (kısayolu değiştirme dahil) ve Kapat düğmeleri var. Boşta bir süre kalınca gözlerini kapatıp işlemciyi yormadan bekliyor.

### Download

- `SweaxMorph-win-x64.zip`: everything included. Unzip and run `SweaxMorph.exe`; it installs itself for your user (no admin) and replaces an older version.
- `SweaxMorph-lite.exe`: smaller, needs the [.NET 9 Desktop Runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/9.0).
